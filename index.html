About Eden

Eden is a rock, blues, and pop band from the Pacific Northwest, bringing an energetic and impactful sound to every performance. Led by Savanna Woods, their music combines original songs with dynamic covers from the 60s through today.

The Band

  • Savanna Woods Vocals  ·  Top 20, NBC’s The Voice
  • Aaron Hiebert Electric Guitar
  • Jason Edwards Drums
  • Ethan Gibbons Bass
II

Our Sound

Eden plays a wide range of music, from original tracks to timeless covers. Whether it is Fleetwood Mac, AC/DC, or Stevie Ray Vaughan, the band guarantees a show that gets you moving.
